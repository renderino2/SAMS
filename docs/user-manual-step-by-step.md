# SAMS Step-by-Step User Manual
## How to Operate the System

**System:** Student Assistant Management System (SAMS)  
**Roles covered:**
- **USER** = Student Assistant
- **ADMIN** = Office Head
- **SUPERADMIN** = HR

---

# PART A — COMMON (ALL USERS)

## A1. How to Log In

1. Open your web browser (Chrome, Edge, or Firefox recommended).
2. Go to the SAMS login page.
3. Enter your **Email Address**.
4. Enter your **Password**.
5. Click **Login**.

**If you are a Student Assistant (USER):**
6. When the browser asks for **location**, click **Allow**.
7. Wait for the system to verify your location.
8. If login succeeds, you are redirected to the **SA Dashboard**.

**If you are Office Head (ADMIN):** you go to **Office Head Dashboard**.  
**If you are HR (SUPERADMIN):** you go to **HR Dashboard**.

---

## A2. How to Log Out

1. Look at the **top bar** (upper right area).
2. Open your account/profile menu.
3. Click **Logout**.
4. You return to the login page.

---

## A3. How to Update Account Settings

1. Click **Account Settings** in the left menu.
2. Update any allowed fields (name, email, contact, address, photo, password).
3. Click **Save** or **Update** at the bottom of the form.
4. Wait for the success message.

**Note:** Student ID, Role, and Office are usually read-only for Student Assistants.

---

# PART B — USER (STUDENT ASSISTANT)

## B1. Daily Start — Login and Go to Attendance

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Log in (see Part A1). Allow location when prompted. |
| 2 | From the left menu, click **Dashboard** to see your summary. |
| 3 | Click **Attendance** to record your work time. |

---

## B2. How to Time In (Start Work)

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Open **Attendance** from the left menu. |
| 2 | Check your name, ID, and office at the top. |
| 3 | Click the **Time In** button. |
| 4 | A camera window opens — **take your photo** (required). |
| 5 | Click **Confirm & Submit**. |
| 6 | Wait for success. **Today's Record** shows your time in. |
| 7 | Status changes to show you are logged in for that session. |

**Important:** You cannot time in again until you complete time out for the current open session (unless starting a new session after a completed one).

---

## B3. How to Time Out (End Work)

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Open **Attendance**. |
| 2 | Click **Time Out** (enabled only after time in). |
| 3 | Take your **time-out photo** when prompted. |
| 4 | Click **Confirm & Submit**. |
| 5 | Check **Total Time Rendered (Today)** on the page. |

**Reminder:**
- Minimum expected hours: **5 hours per day**.
- If you work **more than 5 hours**, the system may show **Request Overtime** — submit overtime approval before timing out if prompted.

---

## B4. How to Request Overtime (If Shown)

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | On the **Attendance** page, when hours exceed 5, click **Request Overtime**. |
| 2 | Fill in the required details in the prompt/form. |
| 3 | Submit the request. |
| 4 | Wait for HR/Office Head approval before completing time out (if required by the system). |

---

## B5. How to View Attendance History

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Open **Attendance**. |
| 2 | Scroll to the **Attendance History** table. |
| 3 | Review date, time in, time out, photos, and status. |
| 4 | Click photo buttons to view time-in or time-out images. |

---

## B6. How to Submit a Request

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Click **Requests** in the left menu. |
| 2 | Scroll to the **Submit Request** form. |
| 3 | Select **Request Type**: Schedule Adjustment, Change Office, Leave Request, or Resignation. |
| 4 | If Resignation, select a **reason** from the dropdown. |
| 5 | Type your **message** explaining the request. |
| 6 | Click **Submit Request**. |
| 7 | Check **Submitted Requests** table — status should be **Pending**. |

---

## B7. How to Track Your Requests

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Open **Requests**. |
| 2 | View the **Submitted Requests** table. |
| 3 | Check columns: type, date, status (**Pending** / **Approved** / **Rejected**), remarks. |
| 4 | If rejected, read remarks and submit a new request if needed. |

---

## B8. How to Add a Skill (Skill Inventory)

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Click **Skill Inventory** in the left menu. |
| 2 | Fill in **Skill Name**. |
| 3 | Add a **Note** (optional description). |
| 4 | Upload **Proof File** (optional but recommended). |
| 5 | Click **Submit** or **Add Skill**. |
| 6 | Your skill appears with status **Pending** until reviewed. |

---

## B9. How to Check Skill Status

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Open **Skill Inventory**. |
| 2 | View your skills list. |
| 3 | Check status: **Pending**, **Approved**, or **Rejected**. |
| 4 | Read reviewer remarks if shown. |

---

## B10. Student Assistant — End of Day Checklist

- [ ] Time In recorded with photo  
- [ ] Time Out recorded with photo  
- [ ] Overtime requested (if applicable)  
- [ ] Any leave/schedule requests submitted  
- [ ] Profile/contact info updated in Account Settings if changed

# PART C — ADMIN (OFFICE HEAD)

## C1. Start of Day — Login and Dashboard

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Log in with Office Head credentials. |
| 2 | You land on **Office Head Dashboard**. |
| 3 | Review summary cards (SAs, attendance, pending items). |
| 4 | Use the left menu to open the module you need. |

---

## C2. How to Monitor and Review DTR (Attendance)

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Click **DTR Monitoring** in the left menu. |
| 2 | Read the alert: *"You have X pending DTRs to review."* |
| 3 | Use **Filters**: search name/ID, date, office, status. |
| 4 | In **DTR Records** table, find the record to review. |
| 5 | Click **Review** (or the action button on that row). |
| 6 | The **DTR Review** panel opens — check time in, time out, status, photos. |
| 7 | Click photo links to verify time-in/time-out images. |
| 8 | Type **Remarks** (optional). |
| 9 | Click **Approve** or **Reject**. |
| 10 | Click **Cancel** to close without saving. |

---

## C3. How to Export DTR Reports (Office Head)

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Open **DTR Monitoring**. |
| 2 | At the top of **DTR Summary**, click **Export PDF** or **Export DOCX**. |
| 3 | Save the file to your computer. |

---

## C4. How to View SA Performance

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Click **SA Performance** in the left menu. |
| 2 | Browse the list of Student Assistants in your office. |
| 3 | Use filters/search if available. |
| 4 | Open details for a specific SA to review performance data. |

---

## C5. How to Manage SA Accounts (Office Scope)

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Click **SA Account Management** in the left menu. |
| 2 | View the list of SAs assigned to your office. |
| 3 | Click **Edit** on an SA row to update allowed fields. |
| 4 | Change schedule or profile fields as permitted. |
| 5 | Save changes. |

## C6. How to Request Additional SAs from HR

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Open **SA Account Management**. |
| 2 | Find the option to **request additional Student Assistants** (form or button on page). |
| 3 | Enter how many SAs you need and the reason/details. |
| 4 | Submit the request to HR. |
| 5 | Track status in **Requests Review** or wait for HR response. |

## C7. How to Submit an Evaluation

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Click **Evaluation Form** in the left menu. |
| 2 | Select the **Student Assistant** to evaluate. |
| 3 | Fill in evaluation details (nature of work, office, date, etc.). |
| 4 | Rate each criterion (e.g., punctuality, initiative, attitude). |
| 5 | Add **Comments** if needed. |
| 6 | Click **Submit Evaluation**. |
| 7 | Confirm success message. |

---

## C8. How to Edit or Delete an Evaluation

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Open **Evaluation Form**. |
| 2 | Find the evaluation in the list below the form. |
| 3 | Click **View** to read details. |
| 4 | Click **Edit** to change and save. |
| 5 | Click **Delete** to remove (confirm when prompted). |

---

## C9. How to Review SA Requests (Office Head)

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Click **Requests Review** in the left menu. |
| 2 | Filter by status: **Pending**, **Approved**, **Rejected** (optional). |
| 3 | Click a request row to open details. |
| 4 | Read request type and message. |
| 5 | Enter **Remarks** if needed. |
| 6 | Click **Approve** or **Reject**. |
| 7 | SA sees updated status on their Requests page. |

---

## C10. How to Review Skills (Office Head)

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Click **Skill Review** in the left menu. |
| 2 | Filter by **Pending** if needed. |
| 3 | Select a skill entry. |
| 4 | Review skill name, note, and proof file. |
| 5 | Add remarks. |
| 6 | Click **Approve** or **Reject**. |

# PART E — QUICK REFERENCE

## Role vs. Menu Access

| Feature | USER (SA) | ADMIN (Office Head) | SUPERADMIN (HR) |
|---------|-----------|---------------------|-----------------|
| Login + geofencing (SA) | Yes | No | No |
| Time In / Time Out | Yes | No | No |
| Submit requests | Yes | No | Yes (review all) |
| Review DTR | No | Yes | Yes (reports) |
| Submit evaluation | No | Yes | Yes |
| HR evaluation review | No | No | Yes |
| Manage offices | No | No | Yes |
| Manage Office Head accounts | No | No | Yes |
| Manage SA accounts | No | Limited (office) | Yes (all) |
| Contract management | No | No | Yes |
| System reports | No | Export DTR | Full reports |

## Status Meanings

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Pending** | Waiting for reviewer action |
| **Approved** | Request/skill/DTR/evaluation accepted |
| **Rejected** | Not approved; read remarks |

## Common Problems

| Problem | What to do |
|---------|------------|
| SA cannot log in — location | Enable GPS and browser location permission |
| Time Out button disabled | Complete Time In first |
| Request stuck on Pending | Contact Office Head or HR |
| Wrong office on profile | Contact HR to update office assignment |
| Forgot password | Contact HR administrator |
